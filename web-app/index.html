Take turns placing one of your kittens on the bed, in any open space available.

Graduate your kittens into cats by lining up three of your kittens in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

Then line three of your cats in a row to WIN the game!

But it isn’t that simple...

When a piece is added to the board, it “boops” all of the pieces adjacent to it, pushing them one space away, including diagonally.

If a piece is booped off the bed, it is returned to the owner’s pool of kitties.

Cats can only be booped by other cats and you cannot boop a kitty into an occupied space.

If you have no kitties left in your pool at the start of your turn, you may remove one kitty from the bed and return it to your pool. This graduates it to a cat and ends your turn.

You can also win the game by having 8 cats of your colour on the bed.
